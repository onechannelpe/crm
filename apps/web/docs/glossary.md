# Glossary (for users)

- **Cliente** is the record that moves through the commercial workflow. Never
  expose the internal term `lead` to users.
- **Comercio** is the RUC reported in GPV and attribution dashboards.
- **Empresa** is the legal organization, or a company-specific action such as
  adding it to favorites. Do not use it as a generic synonym for cliente or
  comercio.
- **Clientes activos** is the active-assignment limit. **Asignaciones diarias**
  is the number of new client assignments available each day.
- **Límite de sucursal** is inherited by teams. **Límite personalizado** is a
  user-specific exception.
- User-facing copy is Spanish. Product names, file extensions, and technical
  values that users must enter are the only exceptions.
